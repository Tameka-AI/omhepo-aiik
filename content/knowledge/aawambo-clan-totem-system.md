# The Aawambo clan and totem system

One is born from two clans — maternal and paternal. The mother’s clan carries identity. Each clan is known by a totem (an animal, plant or object) treated with respect, not as a personality type.

Prominent clans include Aakwanambwa/Aakwanghwiyo (Dog) and Aakwanambuba/Aakwaniilya (Millet). Full lists and dialect variants are held in the clan profiles.
