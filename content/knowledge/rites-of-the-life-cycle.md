# Rites of the life cycle

Birth, naming, the third-year transition and girls’ initiation (efundula in Oukwanyama, olufuko in Ombalantu) mark a person’s passage into the community. These are celebrations of belonging. Their detailed conduct belongs to the families and communities concerned.
