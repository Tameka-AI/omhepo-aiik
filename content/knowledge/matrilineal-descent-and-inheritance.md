# Matrilineal descent and inheritance

Descent, clan membership and chieftainship pass through the mother’s line. A homestead’s land historically returned to the headman on the owner’s death and was reassigned, usually to the wife’s brother’s son, not the owner’s own son. The father still heads the household.
