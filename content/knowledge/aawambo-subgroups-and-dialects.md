# Aawambo sub-groups and dialects

The Aawambo comprise several closely related communities: Aandonga, Aakwambi (Uukwambi), Aangandjera, Aakwaluudhi, Aambalantu (Ombalantu), Aakolonkadhi, Ovakwanyama, Ovaunda and Ovambadja. Each has its own dialect. Oshindonga and Oshikwanyama are the two written standards. Ombalantu material is usually written in Oshindonga orthography.
