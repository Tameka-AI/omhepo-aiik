# Mahangu and the Aawambo table

Pearl millet (omahangu) is the staple. Sorghum, cowpeas, Bambara groundnuts, pumpkins and gourds accompany it. Omalodu and oshikundu are the everyday fermented drinks. Marula, manketti, bird plum and jackal-berry are gathered fruits.
