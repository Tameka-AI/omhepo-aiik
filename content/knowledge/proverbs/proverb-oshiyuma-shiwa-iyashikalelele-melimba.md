# Oshiyuma shiwa iyashikalelele melimba

*The good container does not stay in the pantry.*

A good vessel is always in use; what is good is naturally sought first. Oshiyuma (clay pot) is also the totem emblem of the Aakwanahungi / Aakwanayuma clan.
