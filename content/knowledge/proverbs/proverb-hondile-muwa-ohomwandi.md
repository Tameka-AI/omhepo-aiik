# Hondile muwa ohomwandi

*Good shade is that of the omwandi tree.*

The omwandi (jackal-berry, Diospyros mespiliformis) is a large shade and fruit tree of the north; its shade stands for the shelter of one's own family. Links to the food-and-trees vocabulary in Rodin (1985).
