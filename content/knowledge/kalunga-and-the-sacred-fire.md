# Kalunga and the sacred fire

Kalunga is the supreme being and creator. In the migration legend recorded by Sckär (1916) via Rodin, Kalunga gave the first family the sacred fire, the sacred water, cattle and sheep. Fire-keeping and the king’s fire are central symbols: if the fire goes out in the king’s kraal, the people withdraw.
